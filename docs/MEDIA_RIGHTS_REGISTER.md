## Rules for future media

Before publishing a new photograph, illustration, video still, logo, article image, competition photograph, or other third-party asset:

1. Record the source and creator where known.
2. Record the copyright owner and lawful basis for publication (owned, assigned, licensed, permission, or another valid basis).
3. Record any licence limits, attribution requirements, expiry, or channel restrictions.
4. If identifiable people appear, confirm any required model/publication consent. For minors, use the appropriate parent/guardian consent where required.
5. Do not treat generated imagery as evidence of a real event, result, person, or achievement.
6. Keep private licences, contracts, consent forms, identity records, and receipts outside the public repository; reference them here with a non-sensitive internal evidence ID.

## Public claims

The existing content-truth check remains the primary guardrail for factual claims. Media rights and factual accuracy are separate release conditions: a photograph can be factually relevant but still lack publication rights, and a licensed image does not prove the accompanying claim is true.
